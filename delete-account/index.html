Account control

Delete your Abide account

You can permanently delete your account directly from the Abide app. This page explains where to find the control and what deletion means.

Delete in the app

Open Abide

Sign in to the account you want to permanently delete.

Open Settings

Go to the Abide Settings screen from your account.

Choose “Delete account”

Review the warning and confirm the permanent deletion request.

What happens after deletion?

Account deletion is intended to permanently remove your Abide account and associated personal information, subject to limited retention that may be necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or backups that expire through normal system processes.

Public content associated with your account may require additional processing to remove from public surfaces. Information may remain temporarily in backups or cached systems.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or the in-app deletion control, contact us at . We may need to verify that you own the account before processing a request.
