Privacy Policy

How Abide handles account information, private and public content, media permissions, and other information used to provide the app.

Before publishing: update assets/config.js with your legal name, support/privacy email, and domain.
Effective: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 16, 2026

Abide (“Abide,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Abide mobile application and related services, how we use that information, when information may be shared, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as your email address, username, display name, profile photo, authentication information, and account preferences.

Information you choose to provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide Bible highlights and notes, journal entries, prayer requests, saved Scripture, study responses, course or quiz activity, community posts, comments, public Bible notes, profile information, and creator or community content.

Public and private content

Abide contains both private and public features. Content marked private is intended to remain accessible only to you, except where access is necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or legally comply with the service. Content you intentionally make public may be visible to other Abide users.

Camera, microphone, photos, and video

If you use features that require your camera, microphone, photo library, or video library, Abide may request the corresponding device permission. These permissions support features such as recording or uploading media, choosing profile photos, and creator/community content. You can change device permissions in your device settings.

Notifications and technical information

If enabled, we may process a notification token and notification preferences. We may also process limited technical information needed to operate and secure Abide, such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, crash information, feature interactions, and authentication or security events.

2. How we use information

  • Create and maintain accounts.
  • Provide Bible reading, study, prayer, journal, community, and progress features.
  • Save highlights, notes, prayers, preferences, and learning progress.
  • Display content you choose to make public.
  • Process media you choose to upload.
  • Send requested notifications.
  • Detect abuse, spam, fraud, and security problems.
  • Moderate public content and improve app performance and reliability.

3. AI-assisted features

Certain Abide features may use artificial intelligence or automated systems for study assistance, explanations, recommendations, summaries, or related generated content. Information submitted to an AI-assisted feature may be processed as necessary to generate the requested response. AI-generated material may contain errors and should not be treated as authoritative professional advice.

4. How information may be shared

Service providers

We may use service providers that help operate Abide, including hosting, database, authentication, storage, analytics, communications, content processing, and infrastructure providers. Abide may use Supabase for backend infrastructure, authentication, database, and storage functionality.

Content you make public

Public Bible notes, public prayer requests, community posts, comments, public profile information, creator content, and other content you intentionally publish may be visible to other users.

Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, protect users or the public, investigate abuse or security incidents, or protect the rights and safety of Abide and others.

5. Community safety and moderation

Public content may be reviewed, filtered, restricted, removed, or moderated to maintain a safe community and enforce our rules. Users may be able to report content or accounts and block other users.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accounts, protect security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete or de-identify it.

7. Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your Abide account from within the app. See our Delete Account page for instructions. We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children and teen users

Abide is intended for users age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without authorization required by applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 has provided personal information may contact us.

10. Your choices

Depending on the feature, you may update your profile, adjust notification preferences, control device permissions, choose whether certain notes are public or private, delete content, block or report users, and delete your account.

11. Third-party services and licensed content

Abide may contain links or content provided by third parties. Bible translations, audio, study materials, images, or other content may be provided or licensed by third parties and may be subject to additional rights and terms.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Abide changes. If changes are material, we may provide notice through the app, email, or another appropriate method.
